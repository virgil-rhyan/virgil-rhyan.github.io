Privacy Policy for Virgil

Virgil is a private assistant used only by its owner, Rhyan Garey. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, Virgil reads and manages the owner's own Google Calendar, Gmail and Google Drive so it can schedule events, send reminders and summarize email.

How data is used

Data is used only to provide reminders, scheduling and summaries to the owner. It is not sold, shared with third parties for advertising, or used for any other purpose.

Where data goes

Requests are processed on the owner's private server and by the AI provider that powers the assistant, solely to carry out the owner's requests.

Revoking access

The owner can remove Virgil's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
